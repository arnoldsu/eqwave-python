# Provenance and validation

This workflow follows the Wheeler–Kiladis space–time spectrum and equatorial-wave diagnostics used by NCAR/NCL `diagnostics_cam.ncl` and `kf_filter.ncl`.

No data are included. Before redistribution, confirm licensing and attribution requirements for the source diagnostics and all datasets. Exact bit-for-bit NCL reproduction is not claimed; validate results for each grid and calendar.
